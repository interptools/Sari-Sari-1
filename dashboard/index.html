⚠ Danger zone — erase test data from the cloud

For sample/test transactions made while setting up, before the store goes live. This permanently deletes every sale, stock movement, point movement and approval record for this store from Supabase — on every till, not just this screen. It does not touch your product catalogue, customers, discounts or terminal list; stock simply reverts to the count from your last catalogue push.

Once the store is live, do not use this. Real sales records should never be deleted — void or return them at the till instead, which keeps the audit trail intact. This action cannot be undone.
